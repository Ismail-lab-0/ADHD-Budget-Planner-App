Budget and Planner

Open your app

Tap to open https://ismail-lab-0.github.io/ADHD-Budget-Planner-App/app-x7k2m9/
  1. Tap the link on the device you’ll actually use it on.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen.
    iPhone & iPad — Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
    Android — Chrome → Menu → Add to Home screen
  3. Enter your current balance and your next payday.

That’s it. Once it’s on your Home Screen it opens like any other app and works with no internet.

Keeping the file

The ZIP contains the app as a single HTML file. Unzip it and open the file in any browser.

A note on phones: opening a local file can’t be added to your Home Screen, and Safari sometimes doesn’t keep local-file data between sessions. For phone use, the link on page 1 is the reliable option. The file is here for anyone who’d rather keep the app on their own machine.

Your data stays on your device either way. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
